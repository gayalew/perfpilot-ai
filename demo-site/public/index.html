From the highlands

Each lot is roasted in Bahir Dar the morning it is brewed. The notes below are from this season’s buying trips — the page is long on purpose, so the gallery sits well below a phone screen.

  1. Yirgacheffe, lot 14

    Jasmine and bergamot, washed at the cooperative and dried on raised beds. We brew it as a pour-over, a little cooler than the espresso.

  2. Guji, Uraga

    Stone fruit and cane sugar. The farmers pick in two passes so the cherries that make this lot are uniformly ripe.

  3. Sidama, Bensa

    Black tea and cocoa nib. A longer roast than the Yirgacheffe, still stopped before the second crack runs away.

  4. Limu, natural

    Blueberry and dark honey. We only buy the natural process when the drying beds stay below the afternoon rain.

  5. Harar, longberry

    The oldest cultivated trees we buy from. Blueberry jam in the cup, best as a slow cold brew.

  6. Bench Maji

    A small wild lot. Floral, then a cocoa finish. Roasted in half-kilo batches so it never sits overnight.

  7. Kaffa, forest

    Picked inside the forest the coffee came from. Cedar, lemon zest, and a clean finish.

  8. Jimma, washed

    The everyday espresso. Brown sugar, roasted almond, and enough body to cut with a splash of milk.

  9. Nekemte

    Red apple and malt. A lighter development, aimed at filter brews and the morning pour-over.

  10. Wellega

    Baking spice and orange peel. We keep a bag behind the bar for the pastry pairing this week.

  11. Agaro

    Tropical and sweet, dried in the cherry. The ferment is short so it stays bright rather than boozy.

  12. Gedeb

    Peach and black tea. One of the highest lots of the season, and the one we are proudest of.

  13. Worka

    Florals up front, then a syrupy body. Brewed on the Kalita when the bar is quiet.

  14. Idido

    Lemon candy and white flowers. A small lot; it usually sells out before the weekend.

  15. Chelchele

    Honey process, dried slowly. Tastes like ripe mango and then cocoa.

  16. Hambela

    The last stop on the buying trip. Brown butter and red berries, roasted a touch darker for milk drinks.
